## What MadaRent is

MadaRent is the property platform built for Madagascar, and it is on its way
to becoming the country's leading one. It started with long-term rentals in
Antananarivo and now covers apartments, houses, villas, studios and
commercial space across the main cities: Antananarivo, Toamasina, Mahajanga,
Fianarantsoa, Antsiranana and Toliara.

- **600+ listings online**, updated every day and verified before they go live.
- **Search that fits Madagascar**: by neighbourhood, budget, rooms, car or motorbike access, amenities.
- **A map of every neighbourhood** and its listings.
- **Alerts** the moment a new listing matches what you want.
- **The MadaRent rent index**: the median rent per city and neighbourhood, published every quarter.
- **Free** on the web and on Android, in French, Malagasy and English.

## What it solves

Finding a home in Madagascar has meant scrolling endless posts, chasing
listings that are already gone, and asking for every price. Owners and
agencies have had no proper tool to reach tenants.

MadaRent brings it all into one trusted place: renters find a home fast, and
owners and agencies get a professional space to publish, manage and fill
their properties.

## Where it is going

- **Buy and sell**: land, houses, apartments and commercial property.
- **Tools for agencies and owners**: listings, teams, leads and performance.
- **Property management**: rent collection and deposits through mobile money.
- **Market data**: the rent index today, property valuation next.
